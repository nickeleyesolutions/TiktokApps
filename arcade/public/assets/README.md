Drop background images or looping videos here (jpg, png, webp, gif, mp4, webm).
They show up in the director under Look > backdrop. The CRT/bloom pass is applied
on top, so any art you add takes on the arcade look. Files here are not committed.

Tips: dark images work best (the game dims them by `backdrop.dim`), and slow,
low-detail motion compresses better on stream than busy footage.
